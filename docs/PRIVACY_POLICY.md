Quiet Launcher is provided by [Developer name]. Contact: [Support email].

Quiet Launcher operates offline. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs or network permission. The developer does not collect or share data through the app.

## Information used on your device
Quiet queries launchable app names and components to display your app list. It stores your favorites, hidden-app choices, renamed labels, opening-pause settings, appearance preferences and focus-session timing in private app storage. It does not inspect screen content, messages, notifications, browsing history or time spent inside other apps. It includes no Accessibility service or usage-access monitoring.

Settings remain until you change them, clear Quiet's storage through Android settings, or uninstall Quiet. Android backup is disabled. Clear storage or uninstalling removes Quiet's settings but does not delete your other apps or their files.

## Information you choose to share
If you tap Copy setup details, Quiet copies its version, phone manufacturer/model, Android version, work-profile status and Home-app configuration to your clipboard. Nothing is sent automatically. If you paste these details or email a screenshot to support, the recipient receives the information you choose to send. Review it before sharing. The developer must describe their support-email retention and handling before publishing this policy.

## Your choices
You can change the default Home app through Android settings at any time. Focus and opening pauses apply only to launches through Quiet; essential apps remain available. Android and Google Play process information under their own policies.

## Changes
Update this policy and its effective date if the app's data handling changes.
